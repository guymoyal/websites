# aibuzz.world — Affiliate Earning Strategy

_Last updated: 2026-06-24_

## The honest situation

Affiliate income = **Traffic × Relevance × Conversion × Commission**. Today aibuzz is weak on the two biggest levers:

- **Traffic:** 26 articles, near-zero visitors. No link setup earns without readers — this is the #1 bottleneck.
- **Inventory/relevance:** Admitad mostly carries travel/retail/physical-goods merchants, not the AI SaaS your articles are about. Only **1 of ~3,520 programs is connected** (AliExpress), which is why earnings are ~$0.

**Conclusion:** Admitad should be a *supplementary* layer, not the strategy. The real money for an AI-tools blog is **off-Admitad** (PartnerStack + Impact + direct programs, mostly *recurring* commissions), plus **AdSense** for traffic-based income, plus **growing traffic**.

## The plan (priority order)

1. **Join PartnerStack + Impact** (one approval each → dozens of AI brands). See the off-Admitad list below.
2. **Apply to ~8-10 direct programs** that are open to small sites (Writesonic, ElevenLabs, Rytr, Fliki, Grammarly, Hostinger, Koala/Frase).
3. **Connect the ~40 genuinely-relevant Admitad programs** below (search each name on your [Admitad catalog](https://store.admitad.com/en/webmaster/websites/2951457/catalog/) and click Connect). Skip the ~100 junk merchants (golf, plants, car parts).
4. **Keep AdSense** (already being added) — pays on traffic regardless of relevance.
5. **Grow traffic** — write more, SEO the existing 26. The pruning we did (3,520 → 243 pages) protects the domain from thin-content penalties so traffic can grow.
6. **Re-run the in-content linker** once programs are connected: `yarn partners:fetch` (refresh connected status) → `yarn partners:link-articles` → rebuild. Then every matching article gets earning links automatically.
